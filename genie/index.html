Campaign questions, answered in the time it takes to ask.

Account teams used to file a ticket and wait on whoever could write the SQL. We trained Databricks Genie on the questions they actually ask, wired it into Slack, and built it on metric views so everyone in the organization gets the same answer to the same question.

See what you can ask See the scope

Three pieces, one answer

It looks like a chat message. Underneath, three systems make sure the answer is right, fast, and where people already work.

01

Two trained Genie spaces

118 curated SQL training queries across two spaces: one for Beltone, one covering CounselEAR, Sycle, and Blueprint. Each query ships with metadata, measures, filters, and usage guidance so Genie answers like an analyst who knows the business.

02

A supervisor agent

Reads each question, resolves location names however people type them, picks the right Genie space, and can generate a full scorecard on demand. Five agents working as one.

03

Slack delivery

Genie answers live from Databricks and the answer lands straight in Slack — one platform, end to end. Account teams ask in the channel they already live in, and answers stream back formatted and sourced.

What it covers, and what it leaves alone

Clear boundaries are what make the answers trustworthy.

In scope

Campaign performance

Response, appointments, shows, sales, cost and ROI at location, owner, company, or network level, across the whole network.

Mailing pressure and waste

How often each address gets mailed, where extra touches stop paying, and what gets spent on people who never respond.

Markets and competition

Response by distance from the clinic, competitor density, and untapped prospect pools by tier.

Trends over time

Year over year comparisons and monthly performance trends, with maturity flags on young data.

Out of scope, on purpose
