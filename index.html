01

Two trained Genie spaces

119 curated SQL training queries across two spaces: one for Beltone, one covering CounselEAR, Sycle, and Blueprint. Each query ships with metadata, measures, filters, and usage guidance so Genie answers like an analyst who knows the business.

02

A supervisor agent

Reads each question, resolves location names however people type them, picks the right Genie space, and can generate a full scorecard on demand. Five agents working as one.

03

Slack delivery

No logins, no dashboards. Account executives ask in the channel they already live in, and answers stream back formatted and sourced.
